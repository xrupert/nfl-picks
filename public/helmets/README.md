# Team helmet images (optional)

Drop a helmet image here for any team and the app will use it automatically,
falling back to the built-in original SVG helmet for any team without a file.

## Rules

- **File name = team abbreviation, uppercase, `.png`.** Examples:
  `ARI.png`, `ATL.png`, `BAL.png`, … `WSH.png` (full list below).
- Use **side-profile, facing right** images (facemask on the right) so they
  match the rest of the UI.
- **Transparent background** (PNG) looks best on the dark theme.
- Square-ish images render best (they're drawn with `object-fit: contain`).
- They must be images you have the right to use. Files dropped here are loaded
  as-is; nothing is generated or copied for you.

## All 32 abbreviations

AFC: BAL CIN CLE PIT · HOU IND JAX TEN · BUF MIA NE NYJ · DEN KC LV LAC
NFC: CHI DET GB MIN · ATL CAR NO TB · DAL NYG PHI WSH · ARI LAR SF SEA

After adding files, just refresh the app — no rebuild needed in dev.
